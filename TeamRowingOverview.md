There are two ways to organize a team rowing event.

## 1) Users log in manually

Use this when your users already use Ergometer Space and can organize a lot themselves. For example, when you row with your own team that has used Ergometer Space for some time, or when you want to try out team rowing.

 [Team rowing tutorial](TeamRowing.md)

## 2) Users log in with direct login links
This method makes organizing much simpler and saves the users (rowers) a lot of work. You send each user a link (URL). The link logs the user directly into the right room and team, and gives the user a dashboard that suits their role.

 [Team rowing with login links tutorial](TeamRowingLoginLinks.md)

## Observer or audience
* An **observer** is like a coach: they log in to the room and see the full dashboard with all the values of the rowers. Invite only a few observers, because every observer adds network load for the rowers.
* The **audience** is the public: they only want to watch the race, on a simple web page. There can be many people in the audience.

See [Observer or audience?](TeamRowing.md#observer-or-audience) for a comparison.

## Let an audience watch the race
When you create a private room you can turn on **Public audience**. You then get a link that you can share with anyone, for example family, friends or the people at your event. With this link they can watch the race live in a web browser: the boats, the traffic light and the results. They don't need to log in or have an ergometer. See [Public audience](TeamRowing.md#public-audience) in the team rowing tutorial.
